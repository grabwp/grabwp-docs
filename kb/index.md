**Short answer:** this Knowledge Base explains how GrabWP works and what to do when something goes wrong. It is written for site owners, platform operators and support agents. For step-by-step instructions, use the [documentation](/).

## How the KB differs from the documentation

- The **documentation** tells you what to click, in order.
- The **Knowledge Base** tells you what a thing is, why it matters, where to look in wp-admin and what the safe action is. It links to the documentation when steps are needed.

Every page starts with a short answer you can quote on its own, and lists the plugin versions it was checked against.
