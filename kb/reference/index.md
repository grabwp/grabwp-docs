**Short answer:** these seven lookup tables list the exact UI labels, defaults, status badges, error messages, email templates, shortcodes, blocks and menu paths in GrabWP. Use them to check what a setting defaults to or where a message comes from.

## Pages in this section

- [Notices and error messages](/kb/reference/admin-notices-and-errors): exact admin notices and error strings grouped by plugin, with their cause and troubleshooting link.
- [Base settings](/kb/reference/base-settings): every setting on **Tenancy > Settings** with its tab, default and WordPress constant.
- [Pro settings](/kb/reference/pro-settings): every setting on **Tenancy > Pro Settings** with its tab, default and options.
- [WaaS settings](/kb/reference/waas-settings): every setting on **Waas > Settings** with its tab, default and currency options.
- [Statuses](/kb/reference/statuses): subscription, suspension, provisioning, invoice, service, license and discount statuses, plus backup and clone step lists.
- [Emails, shortcodes and blocks](/kb/reference/emails-shortcodes-and-blocks): the 18 email and page templates, 6 shortcodes, 5 blocks, webhook routes and cron hooks.
- [Where things are in wp-admin](/kb/reference/menu-map): menu and tab map for every plugin, including hidden pages reached from a tenant row.
